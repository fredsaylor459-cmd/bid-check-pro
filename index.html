⚠️ SOME CONCERNS
This roofing quote is in the right ballpark, but a few things need clarification before you sign.

The total price of $14,200 for a 2,400 sq ft roof replacement is within the typical range for your area. However, the quote asks for a 50% deposit upfrontA deposit is money you pay before any work starts. 50% upfront is considered a red flag — reputable contractors usually ask for 10-25% to start, with the rest paid as work progresses. — which is higher than the industry standard. The scope of workThe scope of work is the detailed list of everything the contractor will do: what materials they'll use, how much of it, and exactly what labor is included. Vague scope descriptions make it easy for costs to creep up later. also doesn't specify the shingle brand or underlayment typeUnderlayment is the protective layer installed under your shingles. There are different grades — synthetic underlayment lasts longer and performs better than felt paper. Your quote should specify which type is included..

⚠️
Large upfront deposit requested
50% deposit ($7,100) before work starts is above the standard 10-25% range. Ask for a milestone payment schedule instead.
🚨
Materials not specified
The quote says "architectural shingles" but doesn't name the brand or wind rating. This leaves room for lower-quality substitutions.
✅
Price is within fair range
$14,200 for this project size is reasonable. Fair range for your area: $12,800–$16,400.
💬 Questions to ask your contractor:
Q1 Can we change the deposit to 20% upfront, 40% at midpoint, and 40% when the job is complete?
Q2 What specific brand and model of shingle are you using, and what's the wind speed rating?
Q3 Is the underlayment synthetic or felt paper?
